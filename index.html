Constitutional Justification Matrix

Constitutional Amendment Protected Principle Administrative Application
Fourth Amendment Security in persons, houses, papers, and effects against unreasonable searches and seizures. Prohibits fishing expeditions, unverified warrants, and warrantless administrative demands without verified probable cause.
Fifth Amendment Right to Due Process; protection against deprivation of life, liberty, or property without due process of law. Requires formal opportunity to cure, verification of claim, and a 14-day due process window before any administrative action.
Ninth Amendment Retention of unenumerated rights by the people. Asserts the inherent right to privacy, freedom of contract, and personal sovereignty not explicitly surrendered to the state.
Tenth Amendment Powers not delegated to the United States are reserved to the States or to the people. Limits federal and state agencies strictly to powers expressly delegated; bars overreach into private affairs.
