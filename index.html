Constitutional Jurisprudence & Defense Matrix

Constitutional Anchor Core Guarantee Application to Administrative Intercourse
Fourth Amendment Security in persons, houses, papers, and effects against unreasonable searches and seizures. Absolute prohibition against fishing expeditions, warrantless administrative probes, and demands lacking verified probable cause under oath.
Fifth Amendment Inviolability of Due Process; protection against deprivation of life, liberty, or property without due process of law. Mandates that all administrative actions provide formal notice, an opportunity to cure, verified substantiation of claim, and a rigorous 14-day due process window.
Ninth Amendment Retention of unenumerated rights by the people. Affirms the inherent, natural rights to personal privacy, private contract, freedom of movement, and economic sovereignty not delegated to the state.
Tenth Amendment Reservation of powers not expressly delegated to the federal or state governments to the people. Strictly limits administrative agencies to their statutory boundaries, nullifying ultra vires (unauthorized) acts and unlegislated bureaucratic rules.
